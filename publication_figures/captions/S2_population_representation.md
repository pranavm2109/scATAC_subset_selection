# S2 — technical working caption
Question: are populations present, proportionally represented, and sufficiently sampled for DA?
178 mm width. A: percentage of all annotated cell types with >=10 target cells and >=20 rest cells, cross-checked against saved DA status. All 24 Kidney, 101 HECA and 78 Fetal types are present at every budget; presence and DA eligibility are distinct. B/C: TV and Jensen–Shannon divergence of subset versus full-atlas proportions, computed from existing counts; JS uses base-2 logarithms (bits), not square-root JS distance. D–F: every population at 2% and 10%; enrichment = subset proportion / full-atlas proportion. Hollow markers mean <10 target cells or <20 rest cells, cross-checked against saved DA status tables. The zero line is equal proportions. G–I: deliberately selected rare-population examples, not an unbiased population summary; hollow rings mark DA ineligibility and the dotted line is 10 target cells. Full names/counts are in S2_rare_examples.tsv. Eosinophilic granulocytes and all other types remain in S2_all_population_metrics.tsv.
Sources: results/cell_type_proportions/peakvi/{atlas}/cell_type_proportions_peakvi.tsv and results/signac_da/{atlas}/{fraction}/signac_da_status.tsv. New summary calculations only; no new selections. Presence does not imply composition fidelity, DA eligibility, or superiority over random sampling. Examples are descriptive and should not be presented as a random sample.
