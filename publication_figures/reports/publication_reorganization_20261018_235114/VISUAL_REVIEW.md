All twelve PDF proofs in `pdf_proofs/` were individually inspected at their native 178-mm publication width: Figure 1, S1, S2, S3, S3b, S4, S5, S6, S6b, S7, S8 and S9. Labels, panel letters, axis ticks and colorbars render legibly; no visible text clipping or overlapping legends were identified. S9 retains the original four-panel arrangement and legend bands. SVG containers parse correctly; PDF fonts are embedded TrueType; PNG resolution is 600 DPI (see `final_export_QA.tsv`).

Accepted supplementary artwork is byte-identical to the originals. Visual review did not trigger any alteration of those exports. S6b has no inline method legend in the accepted artwork; the companion measured-benchmark caption explicitly defines the colored solid and grey dashed lines. This existing dependency on the caption was retained rather than changing the accepted figure. S4/S6/S6b use a new separate measured-results reading guide while preserving their original historical caption template.

Original filenames remain authoritative. The optional sequential mapping in FIGURE_INDEX is not applied to the artwork or existing manuscript references. No universal method advantage, 20-seed random DA result or exact-neighbor-search guarantee is implied. Figure 1 distinguishes DA-ineligible cases from measured zero recovery; S9 panel C shows direction agreement, not effect-size correlation.

The archive, original scientific inputs, reports, memberships and accepted exports remain intact. No deletion was performed. Final manuscript prose, the numbering proposal and the BioRender version/license remain author decisions.
